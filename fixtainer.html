Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About FIXtainer ERP

How does FIXtainer ERP track Preventive Maintenance (PM)?

FIXtainer ERP uses automated calendar scheduling based on asset runtime hours or calendar intervals. It automatically creates work orders and dispatches alerts before PM deadlines arrive.

Can technicians operate offline without internet?

Yes. Technicians can log work order checklist items and capture photos offline. The app automatically syncs data back to the server once cellular connectivity is restored.
